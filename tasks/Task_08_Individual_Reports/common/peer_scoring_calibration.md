# Peer Scoring Calibration (Task 8, 0-5 Scale)

Use these rules to keep scoring fair and comparable across all four individual reports.

## Criterion interpretation rules

- **Technical Quality (25%)**: score on correctness, rigor, and robustness of technical outputs (analysis quality and error control), not quantity of files.
- **Delivery Reliability (20%)**: score on end-to-end commitment follow-through (commitment -> execution -> closure), not role visibility.
- **Integration and Handover Quality (15%)**: score on how cleanly work can be reused by teammates (handoff quality, structure, and continuity).
- **Problem-Solving Under Constraints (15%)**: score on proactive blocker resolution under ambiguity and deadline pressure.
- **Collaboration Quality (15%)**: score on communication quality, responsiveness, and ability to unblock others, not agreement level.
- **Adaptability and Learning (10%)**: score on response quality under changing constraints, tools, and role boundaries.

## Scoring discipline

- Use the same evidence depth for each teammate (at least one concrete cue per person).
- Avoid inflation bias: do not give uniformly high scores without differentiated justification.
- Avoid punishment bias: evaluate contribution quality in context of role scope and project phase.
- Keep narrative tone balanced-professional: clear strengths, one concrete improvement area, one evidence cue.
- Use the weighted contract consistently across all reports (same six criteria and same percentages).

## Recommended score bands

- **4.3-5.0**: consistently high-impact contribution with visible ownership and strong reliability.
- **3.7-4.2**: strong and valuable contribution with moderate variability in scope or visibility.
- **3.0-3.6**: meaningful contribution with clear improvement needs in continuity or depth.
- **<3.0**: persistent delivery/reliability issues (use only with strong evidence).

## Required transparency

- Include self-assessment using the same rubric and evidence standard.
- Keep score rationale aligned with the report narrative and evidence appendix.
