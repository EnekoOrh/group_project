# Task 8 Individual Reflective Report Template (Reusable)

> Replace bracketed prompts with your own content. Keep total length near 2,000 words.
> Use `../common/task8_group_narrative_baseline.md` and `../common/evidence_index.csv` as shared factual context, then personalize the interpretation.

## How to Personalize from the Common Baseline

- Keep shared facts consistent (timeline, milestones, constraints).
- Change the analysis to your own role reality, decisions, and critical lessons.
- Use your own evidence cues (repo anchors and non-repo actions) rather than copying another member's examples.
- Keep peer assessment independent and evidence-based, even if you reuse the common rubric.

## 1. Context and Task 1 Baseline

- What role did you propose in Task 1?
- What did your personality profile suggest about your expected behavior?
- What explicit promises did you make to the team?

Write 2-3 paragraphs explaining your initial baseline and why it matters for Task 8 hindsight.

## 2. Hindsight on Personality Fit (Task 1 vs Reality)

- Which parts of your Task 1 personality interpretation were accurate?
- Which parts were overstated or incomplete?
- How did behavior change under time pressure or technical uncertainty?

Write a critical comparison, not a generic summary.

## 3. My Contribution Across the Project

Use these workstreams with specific examples and dates:
- Leadership/Coordination
- Communication/Meetings
- Reporting
- Technical (Optimization)
- Technical (Abaqus or other late-task contribution)

For each workstream, provide:
- what you did,
- one concrete artifact/event as evidence,
- one critical insight about effectiveness.

## 4. Group Performance and Agile Review

Cover both success and failure points.

Suggested structure:
- What worked (iterations, stand-ups, task flow, adaptation)
- What did not work (ownership clarity, bottlenecks, meeting output quality)
- Consequences for delivery quality/speed
- What to change next time

## 5. Role Evolution (Planned vs Real)

Explain whether the project stayed role-based or became role-fluid.

Required points:
- benefits of role fluidity,
- costs of role fluidity,
- your own role shift and its impact.

## 6. Lessons Learned and Career Transfer

Give concrete future actions:
- process action,
- communication action,
- technical action,
- leadership action.

Tie each lesson to future professional behavior.

## 7. Peer Assessment (0-5, Including Self)

Use the common rubric below:
- Technical Quality
- Delivery Reliability
- Integration and Handover Quality
- Problem-Solving Under Constraints
- Collaboration Quality
- Adaptability and Learning

Include one score table for all members and short professional justification paragraphs for each person (including yourself).

## 8. Conclusion

Close with a precise statement on:
- what Task 1 got right,
- what project reality changed,
- your main development target.

## 9. Timesheet Reconstruction Method (if no formal timesheet was used)

Explain how hours were reconstructed:
- source evidence used,
- confidence labeling method,
- limitations.

## Appendices

- Reconstructed timesheet table (weekly)
- Claim-to-evidence matrix
- Contribution timeline
