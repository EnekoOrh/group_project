# Contribution Timeline (Task 1 Release -> Task 8 Release)

## Phase 1: Role-anchored startup (Oct 2025 -> Dec 2025)
- **Intent from Task 1**: operate as Group Lead with strong planning and communication ownership, while still contributing technically.
- **Observed contribution mode**:
  - setup and coordination support in early meetings and task allocation rhythm,
  - report-quality and requirement-alignment attention during Task 3 delivery,
  - moderate technical contribution in optimization workflows.
- **Contribution shift note**: roles were still relatively aligned with initial declarations.

## Phase 2: Structured technical delivery (Jan 2026 -> Feb 2026)
- **Main context**: Task 4 then Task 6 created stronger pressure on reproducibility, traceability, and report quality.
- **Observed contribution mode**:
  - higher ownership of workflow orchestration (run pipelines, report pipelines),
  - stronger report-writing/completion/correction effort,
  - continued technical contribution in optimization tooling integration.
- **Contribution shift note**: leadership and reporting load increased versus initial role definition.

## Phase 3: Role blur and cross-support (Mar 2026)
- **Main context**: Task 7 (Abaqus) required fast iteration between simulation outputs, post-processing, and reporting quality.
- **Observed contribution mode**:
  - maintained coordination and quality gate role,
  - contributed directly to late-stage Abaqus/report integration,
  - operated in a hybrid mode (leadership + technical + report integration).
- **Contribution shift note**: initial role boundaries became fluid; delivery depended on shared support and overlap.

## Critical interpretation
- The role-anchored start was useful for onboarding clarity.
- The late-stage fluid model improved resilience and speed.
- The tradeoff was weaker ownership separation and heavier load concentration on coordination/report integration.
