\chapter{Introduction}

This report addresses Task 6: optimization of a hyperboloid cooling tower shell.
The optimization goal is to reduce lateral shell area while maintaining required volume and respecting geometrical and constructability constraints.

Three solvers are compared under identical scenario definitions:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item Simulated Annealing (SA),
\item Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO),
\item BFGS (deterministic quasi-Newton).
\end{itemize}

The workflow is fully reproducible from repository scripts.
Numerical results and figures are generated from \texttt{tasks/Task\_06\_Cooling\_Tower/run\_experiments.py}, then formatted for this \LaTeX{} report.
