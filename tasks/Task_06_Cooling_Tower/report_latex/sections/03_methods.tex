\chapter{Optimization Methods}

\section{Simulated Annealing (SA)}
SA performs local perturbations and accepts worsening moves with temperature-controlled probability, enabling basin transitions early and local refinement later.

\section{Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO)}
PSO evolves a particle population using inertia and attraction to personal/global best states.
It is designed for broad exploration of non-convex landscapes.

\section{BFGS}
BFGS uses first-order information to update an inverse Hessian approximation and compute efficient descent steps.
In this task, analytic gradients from the model and penalties are used to improve local convergence speed.
